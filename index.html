Welcome to AllerFoods

Eat with confidence. Order with convenience. Dine with greater security.

AllerFoods connects food allergy awareness, convenient online food ordering, and participating food establishments in one platform. Manage your allergy profile, discover food options, order your favorite meals, and securely share essential allergy information with authorized restaurant staff through your AllerCard.

How AllerFoods Works

  1. 1. Create Your Profile

    Register and securely save your food allergy information in your AllerFoods account.

  2. 2. Discover and Order

    Explore participating food establishments, discover available meals, and conveniently place orders online.

  3. 3. Share Your Allergy Information Securely

    Use your AllerCard QR code to let authorized restaurant staff access the necessary allergy information for your visit.

  4. 4. Partner With AllerFoods

    Are you a food establishment? Join AllerFoods to connect with customers, showcase your menu, manage orders, and support more informed food choices through allergy-aware service.

AllerCard is an information and communication tool. It does not diagnose allergies or guarantee an allergen-free meal.

Before We Collect Your Information

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how AllerEats plans to collect and use your personal information.

What We Collect

Your name, email address, delivery details, food preferences, order information, and food allergy information you provide.

Why We Collect It

To help you discover partner food establishments, browse menus, place and manage orders, personalize your food experience, and provide allergy-related information when needed.

How Your Allergy Information Is Shared

If you choose to use the AllerCard, authorized personnel at participating food establishments may view your relevant allergy information after scanning your card. This helps restaurant staff identify your declared allergies and take them into consideration when handling your order.

How We Protect Your Information

We plan to implement appropriate security measures and access controls to protect your personal information. Your allergy details will not be stored directly in the QR code.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We will retain your information only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by applicable law.

Your Privacy Rights

You may exercise your applicable privacy rights, including requesting access to or correction of your information and requesting its deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Important Food Safety Notice: AllerFoods and AllerCard help communicate food allergy information but cannot guarantee that food is allergen-free or prevent all risks of cross-contact.
