Welcome to AllerFoods

Eat with confidence. Order with convenience. Dine with greater security.

AllerFoods connects food allergy awareness, convenient online food ordering, and participating food establishments in one platform. Manage your allergy profile, discover food options, order your favorite meals, and securely share essential allergy information with authorized restaurant staff through your AllerCard.

How AllerFoods Works

  1. 1. Create Your Profile

    Register and securely save your food allergy information in your AllerFoods account.

  2. 2. Discover and Order

    Explore participating food establishments, discover available meals, and conveniently place orders online.

  3. 3. Share Your Allergy Information Securely

    Use your AllerCard QR code to let authorized restaurant staff access the necessary allergy information for your visit.

  4. 4. Partner With AllerFoods

    Are you a food establishment? Join AllerFoods to connect with customers, showcase your menu, manage orders, and support more informed food choices through allergy-aware service.

AllerCard is an information and communication tool. It does not diagnose allergies or guarantee an allergen-free meal.

Before we collect your allergy information

Under the Data Privacy Act, here is how we handle your data:

  • What: name, email, birthday, allergies, optional emergency notes.
  • Why: to build your AllerCard and show your allergies to partner staff.
  • Who sees it: partner staff see it only after scanning your card, and only for that scan.
  • Protection: encrypted connection, access controls, no allergy data inside the QR code.
  • Retention: kept while your account is active. You can ask for correction or deletion anytime.
