Say your allergy once. Show it everywhere.

AllerPass keeps your food-allergy profile. AllerCard is the QR card that lets participating restaurants see it, so you never have to explain it again.

  1. Register your allergies in AllerPass.
  2. Receive your AllerCard with a unique QR code.
  3. Show it at a partner. Staff scan it and see only what they need, only for that visit.

AllerCard is an information and communication tool. It does not diagnose allergies or guarantee an allergen-free meal.

Before we collect your allergy information

Under the Data Privacy Act, here is how we handle your data:

  • What: name, email, birthday, allergies, optional emergency notes.
  • Why: to build your AllerCard and show your allergies to partner staff.
  • Who sees it: partner staff see it only after scanning your card, and only for that scan.
  • Protection: encrypted connection, access controls, no allergy data inside the QR code.
  • Retention: kept while your account is active. You can ask for correction or deletion anytime.
